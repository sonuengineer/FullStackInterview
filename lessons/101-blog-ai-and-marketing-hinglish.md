# Blog: AI Almost Kuch Bhi Solve Kar Leta Hai - Phir Marketing Itni Mushkil Kyun Hai? (Hinglish)

## Sawaal

AI code likh deta hai, design bana deta hai, copy bhi likh deta hai. Phir bhi marketing "solve" nahi hui. Log ab bhi launch karte hain aur silence milti hai. Kyun?

## Jawaab: Marketing ka bottleneck **output** nahi, **attention aur trust** hai

Engineering mein problem *closed* hoti hai: "ye API 200ms mein response de" - sahi ya galat check ho sakta hai. Marketing ki problem *open* hai: "logon ko parwaah kyun ho?" Iska koi ek sahi jawab nahi, aur jawab time ke saath badalta rehta hai.

Teen wajah:

**1. Attention zero-sum hai.** Code likhna non-zero-sum hai - main likhun ya aap, dono ka code chal sakta hai. Par ek user ke din mein utne hi minutes hain. AI sabko tez bana deta hai, isliye **supply** badh jaati hai, demand utni hi rehti hai. Jab sab log roz 10 posts likh sakte hain, to average post ki value girti hai - AI se content sasta hua, *dhyaan* nahi.

**2. Trust shortcut nahi le sakta.** Ek achhi landing page 10 minute mein ban jaati hai. "Ye log paisa lekar bhaag to nahi jaayenge" - ye feeling 10 minute mein nahi banti. Reviews, referrals, case studies, brand - ye sab **time aur repetition** maangte hain. AI likhawat tez karta hai, bharosa nahi.

**3. Feedback loop slow aur shor-bhara hai.** Code: test fail hua to turant pata. Campaign: 2 hafte baad pata chalta hai ki kaam kiya ya nahi, aur us beech season, competitor, algorithm - sab badal gaya. Jahan feedback dheema ho, wahan "AI se 100 guna zyada try karo" bhi kaam nahi karta, kyunki aap seekh hi nahi paate ki kaunsa try chala.

## Isi Wajah Se Ye Bhi Sach Hai

Yehi baat [[55-blog-engineering-harder-building-easier]] wali hai, doosri jagah: **banana sasta ho gaya, isliye jo cheez sasti nahi hui uski keemat badh gayi**. Software mein wo judgment hai; business mein wo distribution hai ([[47-technology-vs-distribution]]).

## Engineer Ke Liye Practical Takeaway

- **Distribution pehle socho, product ke saath.** "Pehle 100 log kaun honge aur main unhe kaise dhoondhunga?" - ye code se pehle ka sawaal hai.
- **Ek channel jeeto, 5 nahi.** Ek jagah bharosa banana 5 jagah shor karne se behtar hai.
- **Repetition boring lagta hai, kaam wahi karta hai.** Aapko apna message 50th baar bolte hue bore hoga, tab jaakar log pehli baar sunenge.
- **AI ko volume ke liye mat use karo, iteration ke liye karo:** 10 versions likhwa lo, par bhejo wahi jo aap khud defend kar sako.

## 🧠 Remember

> AI ne content banana sasta kar diya, par attention aur trust wahi ke wahi limited hain - isliye marketing ab bhi mushkil hai: bottleneck likhne mein nahi, suna jaane aur bharosa jeetne mein hai.

2. Aapke kaam mein aisi kaunsi cheez hai jo AI sasti *nahi* kar sakta?
