# Salary Negotiation: Indian Context Mein Practical Guide

*Is library ka sabse financially consequential lesson. Ek 45-minute conversation 3-5 LPA ka difference bana sakti hai -- aur wo difference har agle switch par compound hota hai, kyunki next company bhi aapke last CTC se shuru karti hai.*

---

## 1. Pehla Number Mat Do

Negotiation ka poora game ek line par ghoomta hai: **jo pehle number bolta hai, wo anchor set karta hai.**

Agar aapne 16 bola, to 18 par baat khatam hogi, chahe budget 26 tha. Recruiter ka kaam hi yahi hai ki wo number pehle aapse nikaale. **Deflect kaise karein:**

> "Main role ke liye right fit hoon ye pehle confirm kar lein -- compensation par main flexible hoon aur market standard follow karta hoon. Aap bata sakte hain is role ke liye band kya hai?"

Agar wo push karein:

> "Aapke paas is level ke liye ek range zaroor hogi -- wo bata dein, main bata dunga ki wo mere liye kaam karti hai ya nahi."

Do baar deflect karna theek hai. **Teesri baar insist karne par number do** -- warna aap difficult lagne lagte ho, aur recruiter aapko screen out kar deta hai. Yahan politeness strategy hai, weakness nahi.

**Jab form number maangta hai:** Naukri/company portal "Expected CTC" ko mandatory field bana deta hai. Options, best se worst:

| Option | Kab |
|---|---|
| `Negotiable` ya `As per company standard` likho | Pehle try karo -- kai forms text accept karte hain |
| Ek **range** do: `24-28 LPA` | Numeric field ho to, range se bachne ki jagah milti hai |
| Ek number do, par **market rate par**, current CTC par nahi | Agar koi choice na ho |
| Khali chhodo | Sirf tab jab optional ho |

Aur dhyan rakho: form par likha number **binding nahi hai.** Baad mein aap bol sakte ho: "wo maine role detail jaanne se pehle bhara tha; ab scope dekhkar mera expectation ye hai."

## 2. Sabse Badi Earnings Mistake: Current CTC Par Percentage

Ye India mein #1 galti hai, aur isse log saalon tak kam kamate hain.

```
GALAT soch:  mera CTC 12 hai -> 40% hike maang lu -> 17 maang doon
SAHI soch:   is role ka market rate 22-26 hai -> main us band mein hoon ya nahi?
```

**Kyun galat hai:** aapka current CTC aapki *value* nahi hai, wo ek **history** hai -- ek pichla negotiation, ek weak appraisal cycle, ya ek company jo kam deti thi. Us galti par percentage lagane se aap wahi galti carry forward kar rahe ho. Company aapko "CTC + 30%" fairness ki wajah se nahi deti -- wo deti hai kyunki wo **sasta** hai. Unka actual budget us role ke liye already decided hai.

**Market rate kaise pata karein:**

| Source | Kaise use karein |
|---|---|
| **AmbitionBox / Levels.fyi / Glassdoor** | Company + role + YOE filter karo. Levels.fyi product companies ke liye best |
| **2-3 recruiter calls** | "Is level par aap kya dekh rahe ho?" -- wo range bata dete hain |
| **Blind / Reddit India threads** | Noisy, par offer-breakup posts honest hote hain |
| **Apne ex-colleagues** | Sabse accurate. Awkward hai, par ek message bhej do |
| **Jo offers aapke friends ko mile** | Same YOE, same city -- yahi aapka real benchmark |

Ek honest baat: range chaudi hoti hai. 5 saal Node backend par Bangalore mein service company 14-18 de sakti hai, mid-size product company 22-30, aur funded product/FAANG-adjacent 35+. **Aap kis bucket mein apply kar rahe ho, ye aapki range decide karta hai** -- aapka current CTC nahi.

## 3. Indian Breakup Samjho -- "CTC" Ek Marketing Number Hai

Offer letter mein 28 LPA likha hai. Haath mein kya aayega?

| Component | Kya hai | Kitna bharosa karein |
|---|---|---|
| **Fixed** | Monthly salary, guaranteed | 100%. **Negotiate yahi karo.** |
| **Variable / Performance bonus** | 10-20%, company + individual rating par | Historical payout poochho. 70-100% normal, 50% bhi hota hai |
| **Retention bonus** | 1-2 saal tak locked, chhodne par clawback | Ye golden handcuff hai -- amount aur clawback clause padho |
| **Joining bonus** | One-time, usually clawback with 1 yr bond | Notice buyout cover karne ke liye useful |
| **ESOP / RSU** | Private company mein paper; listed mein real | Private startup ESOP ko **zero maano** jab tak liquidity event ka track record na ho |
| **Gratuity / PF employer part** | CTC mein add kiya jaata hai, haath mein nahi aata | CTC inflate karne ka tool |
| **"Benefits" (insurance, meal card)** | 50k-1L CTC mein add ho jaata hai | Nice, par salary nahi |

**Do offers, same CTC, bahut alag reality:**

| | Offer A | Offer B |
|---|---|---|
| CTC | 28 LPA | 28 LPA |
| Fixed | 25 | 19 |
| Variable | 3 | 4 (last year 60% paid) |
| Retention (2 yr lock) | 0 | 2 |
| ESOP (private, pre-revenue) | 0 | 3 |
| **Realistic year-1 cash** | **~27** | **~21.4** |

Isliye hamesha poochho: **"Fixed kitna hai?"** CTC nahi. Aur negotiation bhi fixed par karo -- variable badhwa kar aapne kuch nahi jeeta.

## 4. Kya Negotiable Hai, Kya Nahi

| Item | Negotiable? | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Fixed salary | **Haan** -- sabse pehle yahi | 10-15% movement common hai |
| Joining bonus | **Haan** -- aasan "yes" | Budget alag hota hai, isliye HR ko dena easy lagta hai |
| Variable % | Thoda | Band-level policy hota hai, usually locked |
| Level / designation | Kabhi-kabhi | Ye sabse high-leverage hai -- level badla to band badla |
| Joining date | **Haan** | Notice period handle karne ke liye |
| Relocation / laptop | Haan, chhota amount | |
| WFH / hybrid days | Depends, likhwao | Verbal promise manager change hone par mar jaata hai |
| ESOP grant size | Startup mein haan | Strike price, vesting, cliff poochho |
| Notice period (unka) | Nahi | Standard policy |
| Insurance / PF structure | Nahi | Company-wide |
| Appraisal cycle eligibility | Sometimes | "Main April mein join kar raha hoon, March cycle mein eligible hounga?" -- likhwao |

**Level par negotiate karna sabse underrated move hai.** SDE-2 vs SDE-3 ka difference salary se zyada hai -- wo aapka next appraisal, next scope, aur next company ka starting point badal deta hai.

## 5. Competing Offer -- Sabse Strong Position

Ek sach: **negotiation leverage ek hi cheez se aata hai -- alternatives.** Argument se nahi, need se nahi, 3 saal ki loyalty se nahi.

Clean version:

> "Main aapki team ke saath kaam karna chahta hoon, aur technically ye role mere liye best fit hai. Mere paas ek doosra offer hai jiska fixed 26 hai. Agar aap fixed 25 ke aas-paas aa sakein, main aaj hi sign kar dunga."

Kaam kyun karta hai: preference clearly stated hai (aap blackmail nahi kar rahe), ek specific number hai (HR ko approval chain mein le jaane ke liye number chahiye), aur close ka vaada hai ("main sign kar dunga" HR ka actual KPI hai).

**Timing:** offers ko **cluster** karo. 3-4 companies ke process ek saath 2-3 hafte mein chalao taki offers overlap karein. Ek offer haath mein hone par pehla offer bhi 10-15% upar chala jaata hai -- same resume, same skill, bas alternatives ki wajah se.

**Bluff mat karo.** Fake offer bolna India mein particularly kharab idea hai: HR offer letter dikhane ko keh sakta hai (25+ LPA par common), recruiter network chhota hai aur ek hi consultancy do companies ko hire kar rahi hoti hai, BGV mein jhooth nikalne par offer revoke hota hai, aur exposed hone par aapka **real** leverage bhi zero ho jaata hai. Agar competing offer nahi hai to honest leverage use karo -- "mere paas 2 process final stage par hain" (agar sach hai) -- ya merit-based counter (next section).

## 6. Jab Wo Shuru Mein Hike Expectation Poochhein

Classic recruiter line: *"Aap kitne percent hike expect kar rahe ho?"* Ye trap hai, kyunki "percent" sunte hi anchor aapke purane CTC par set ho jaata hai. Isse reframe karo:

> "Main percentage ke bajaye role ke hisaab se sochta hoon. Mera current package mere pichle role ka reflection hai -- ye role zyada ownership wala hai. Is level par market mein mujhe 24-28 ka fixed dikh raha hai, aur main usi band mein baat karna chahta hoon."

Aur agar wo bole "humari policy maximum 30% hike hai":

> "Samajh gaya. Mera sawaal yahi hai ki is role ka internal band kya hai -- agar band mere expectation se match karta hai to hike percentage apne aap theek ho jaayegi. Aur agar band kam hai, to hum dono ka time bach jaayega."

Ye line "30% policy" ko polite tarike se band discussion mein convert kar deti hai.

## 7. Counter Kaise Karein -- Ek Baar, Number + Reason

**Ek clean counter** karo, teen round ka haggling nahi. Formula: *appreciation + commitment + ek specific number + ek reason + close signal.*

> "Thank you, aur main role ke bare mein genuinely excited hoon. Ek cheez par baat karna chahta hoon -- fixed 21 offer hua hai. Is role mein backend ownership ke saath on-call aur AWS cost side bhi hai, aur maine wo apne current role mein kiya hai ([[96-blog-12lpa-to-22lpa-packaging]]). Market mein is scope ke liye mujhe 25-26 dikh raha hai. Agar fixed 25 ho jaaye, main offer accept kar lunga. Baaki structure jaisa hai waisa theek hai."

Rules:
- **Likhit mein (email) counter karo** -- HR ko wo approval chain mein forward karna hota hai. Call par bola hua number kho jaata hai.
- **Ek number do, range nahi** -- counter stage par range do, wo lower end pick karegi.
- **Sirf ek hi cheez par** counter karo. Fixed + joining bonus + WFH + level, sab ek saath maangna offer ko risk mein daalta hai.
- **Reason scope-based ho**, need-based nahi. "Mera rent badh gaya" leverage nahi hai.
- **Deadline respect karo** par artificial urgency mat maano. "Aaj shaam tak batao" par aap "mujhe 48 ghante chahiye, main seriously consider kar raha hoon" keh sakte ho.

## 8. Kab Walk Away Karein

| Signal | Kyun serious hai |
|---|---|
| Fixed itna kam ki 2 saal baad bhi aap market se peeche honge | Compounding ulta chalta hai |
| Offer letter mein bond / 2 saal ka service agreement | Exit option khatam |
| Verbal promise, written mein nahi ("6 mahine mein level badha denge") | Ye promise 70% cases mein nahi hota |
| HR aggressive ya misleading hai | Jo HR offer stage par aisa hai, wo appraisal par kaisa hoga |
| CTC bada hai par fixed chhota aur ESOP par based | Paper par rich, bank mein nahi |
| Resign karne ke baad joining date khisak rahi hai | Red flag -- hiring freeze ka hint |

Walk away karne ki **ability** hi aapki poori leverage hai. Isliye hamesha ek alternative rakho -- doosra offer, ya current job jisse aap turant resign na karo.

## 9. Resign Karne Se Pehle -- Non-Negotiable Checklist

India mein log excitement mein verbal offer par resign kar dete hain, aur phir weeks tak latke rehte hain.

1. **Written offer letter** haath mein ho -- email par PDF, saari components ke saath.
2. **Fixed, variable, joining bonus, retention, clawback** -- sab letter mein likha ho.
3. **Joining date** confirmed ho, aur wo aapke notice period ke baad ho.
4. **Clawback clauses** padhe ho (joining bonus 1 saal, retention 2 saal, etc.).
5. **BGV requirements** clear ho -- jo documents aap de nahi sakte, pehle bata do.
6. **Jo verbal promise hua** (WFH days, level review, appraisal eligibility) wo email par confirm karwa lo. "Jaisa humne discuss kiya..." wala ek email kaafi hai.

Tab resign karo. Isse pehle nahi.

**Notice period:** 30 din (startup/mid-size) vs **90 din (bade service companies)**. 90 din ka notice aapki market value kam karta hai, kyunki companies turant joiner chahti hain. Agar **buyout** allowed hai (1-2 mahine ki salary), naye employer se joining bonus mein wo cover karwane ki request karo -- ye ek easy "yes" hota hai. Aur notice period mein leave adjust hoti hai ya nahi, HR se likhit mein pooch lo.

**Counter-offer current company se:** agar aapka current employer counter de -- 90% cases mein **wo short-term patch hota hai.** Honest sawaal poochho: jo cheez aapko chhodne par majboor kar rahi thi (scope, manager, learning), kya wo paisa badalne se badal rahi hai? Agar sirf salary thi aur wo match ho gayi, to rukna rational ho sakta hai -- par ek baat yaad rakho: aapne dikha diya ki aap market mein ho. Kuch managers us din se aapko "flight risk" treat karte hain, aur aapko critical projects se door rakhte hain.

## 10. Ek Cheez Jo Poore Negotiation Ko Easy Kar Deti Hai

Negotiation ek 45-minute conversation nahi hai -- wo uska **last 5%** hai. Baaki 95% wo hai jo aap mahine pehle karte ho: strong profile, ek system jo aap load ke under explain kar sakte ho, interviews mein trade-off bolna, aur ek saath mein kai processes chalana ([[96-blog-12lpa-to-22lpa-packaging]]). Aur ek strategic baat: aapki skill kitni bhi achhi ho, aapka package largely **wo decide karta hai ki aap kis company mein ho** -- waise hi jaise ek better product ke bajaye better distribution jeet jaata hai ([[47-technology-vs-distribution]]). Same Node.js engineer service company mein 14 par hai aur product company mein 28 par. **Sabse bada "hike" negotiation se nahi, bucket badalne se aata hai.**

## 🧠 Remember

> Pehla number mat do; current CTC par percentage ke bajaye role ke market rate par baat karo; negotiate sirf **fixed** par karo kyunki variable aur private ESOP guarantee nahi hain; leverage sirf ek real alternative se aata hai (fake offer kabhi nahi); ek baar politely counter karo number aur reason ke saath; aur resign karne se pehle sab kuch likhit mein lo.

## Quick Self-Test

1. Recruiter pehle call par CTC poochhta hai -- aapki exact do lines kya hongi?
2. Do offers, dono 28 LPA CTC. Kaunse 4 sawaal poochhkar aap decide karoge ki asli mein kaun bada hai?
3. "Humari policy 30% hike se zyada nahi" -- aapka jawab?
4. Private pre-revenue startup 6 LPA ka ESOP de raha hai. Aap use apne calculation mein kitna count karoge aur kyun?
5. Verbal offer aa gaya, team mast hai. Resign karne se pehle kaun si 6 cheezein confirm karni hain?
